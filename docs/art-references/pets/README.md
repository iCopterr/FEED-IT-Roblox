# Roblox-style pet concept references

Generated with the built-in image generation tool for the owner to model manually.
Four sheets cover the 24 configured pets in Advanced, Elite, Master and Supreme.
Basic Egg is excluded at owner request. These are visual concepts, not meshes,
orthographic blueprints, topology guarantees or approved production replacements.

All species names and broad silhouettes were visually checked against PetConfig.
Use one shared low-poly style, merge geometry where practical, and paint small
markings instead of creating separate parts. Multihead/wing counts and hidden
anatomy need a deliberate modeling pass; generated images are not exact anatomy.
The owner has paused agent model creation after rejecting the Blender draft.
No production models, UI or gameplay were changed.

# Prompt set

+## Advanced Egg

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a polished landscape 3D GAME MODEL REFERENCE SHEET for a human building pets in Roblox Studio/Blender. Exactly six distinct pets in an evenly spaced 3-column by 2-row grid on a plain pale warm gray background. Every cell contains ONE large complete three-quarter view of the creature, all feet/tails/wings visible, and its exact name below. Title across top. Consistent recognizable ROBLOX low-poly style: chunky softly bevelled cuboid heads, simple wedge ears, short sturdy limbs, large expressive flat black eyes with small white highlights, clean readable silhouettes, matte flat colors, broad planar surfaces, tasteful cute-adventurous proportions. Not hyperrealistic, not detailed fantasy illustration, not plush toys, not Minecraft pixel textures. NO surface studs, no separate microgeometry, no individual fur strands, no chains/jewelry, no busy background, no props, no floating effects, no pedestals. Designs must LOOK practical to build with a few merged meshes; do not write part counts on the image. Keep anatomical connections cohesive and limbs legible, avoid excessive horns/spikes. Soft neutral studio lighting. Make all six animals visually distinct but from one game art direction.
Title: Advanced Egg. Pets in reading order: Ember Drake (small terracotta dragon orange accents broad wings); Frost Wolf (white ice-blue wolf chunky mane); Storm Griffin (slate blue eagle-lion with yellow accents broad wings); Lava Golem (bulky charcoal rock creature simple orange cracks painted on its surface); Thunder Serpent (coiled yellow and dark blue serpent two broad angular fins); Ice Dragon (pale cyan dragon white broad horns and wings)
